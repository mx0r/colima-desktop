- **One copy at a time.** Starting the app again while it runs opens its menu instead of adding a second icon to the menu bar.
- **Exact durations.** Uptime and other durations show their two largest units, such as 2d 4h, 3h 12m, 5m 30s or 42s, and count up while the menu is open.
- **Tidier container menus.** A container with more than one port lists its ports in a Ports submenu, and more than six containers go into a Containers submenu.
