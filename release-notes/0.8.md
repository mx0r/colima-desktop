- **Colima Desktop window.** Open Colima Desktop in the menu shows the containers (filterable, with their actions and details) and the environment in one window, with a menu bar. Starting the app again shows it too.
- **New Container…** in the menu: search Docker Hub, pick a tag, set the name, command, restart policy, ports, environment variables and volumes in a form, and create the container. A missing image is pulled with progress, and the logs and a terminal are one click away. Images from other public registries work by name; Settings → Image sources sets where to search.
- **Quitting asks about Colima.** Quitting leaves Colima and its containers running; the app now says so and offers Stop Colima and Quit. "Don't ask again" remembers the answer, and Settings → General → Reset Confirmations brings the question back.
- **Exact durations.** Uptime and other durations show their two largest units, such as 2d 4h, 3h 12m, 5m 30s or 42s, and count up while the menu is open.
- **Tidier container menus.** A container with more than one port lists its ports in a Ports submenu, and more than six containers go into a Containers submenu.
- **One copy at a time.** Starting the app again while it runs shows its window instead of adding a second icon.
- **Update notice.** After an update installs, a notification says which version runs now.
